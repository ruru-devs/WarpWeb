# WarpWeb: Commercial Sales Playbook & Pitch Deck

## 1. The Core Commercial Pitch (For Business Owners)

### The Problem
- **70%+ of website drop-offs** occur not because the business lacks the service or product, but because the visitor **couldn't locate it within 10 seconds** through messy nested menus, long pages, or PDFs.
- Standard chatbots are clunky, slow (taking 5-8 seconds per answer), hallucinate, and merely spit generic text without actually taking the user anywhere.

### The Solution: WarpWeb
- **"Stop Navigating, Start Arriving"**: Type any natural intent into a `<15KB` widget, and get instantly transported (with smooth scroll and pulse highlight) to the exact section, doctor profile, price chart, or booking form in **under 150ms**.
- **The Findability & Lost Visitor Report**: The dashboard reveals the queries visitors typed that had zero matches on their site. This pinpoints the exact revenue-generating services or answers the business is missing, while turning lost visitors into warm leads via one-click WhatsApp/Callback capture.
